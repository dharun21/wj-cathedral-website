# Operations, limits and cost planning

For review and a low-traffic launch, Supabase Free plus static hosting may be sufficient, subject to current provider quotas. Verify official pricing before launch because quotas change. Free projects may pause after inactivity and do not promise paid-tier uptime, support, backup or recovery. Do not create artificial keep-alive traffic.

Software services may initially cost ₹0 while usage stays within free quotas. Domain renewal, project-owned email/SMTP, taxes and optional paid Supabase service are separate costs.

Monitor monthly: database size, Storage, media egress, API traffic, Auth email usage, failed login/recovery attempts, hosting bandwidth, backup completion and restore-test date.

Upgrade before the site becomes operationally critical, when quotas approach 70–80%, when pausing is unacceptable, or when stronger backups/support/availability are required.

The church should own Supabase, domain, hosting, billing, recovery email and MFA devices. The developer may have least-privilege access but should not be the sole owner.

Known boundaries: no analytics counters, public registration, payments, ticketing, prayer-request storage or online student application collection. YouTube “live” status is manual. Live security testing requires the owner-connected project.
